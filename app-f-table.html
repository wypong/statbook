    Appendix F The F Distribution

    This appendix gives right-tail critical values for the F distribution. Each entry \(F_\alpha(d_1,d_2)\) satisfies \(P(F>F_\alpha)=\alpha\text{,}\) where \(d_1\) is the numerator degrees of freedom and \(d_2\) is the denominator degrees of freedom.
    🔗
    Because the full \(F\) table is large, the values are split into smaller tables by significance level and numerator degrees of freedom.
